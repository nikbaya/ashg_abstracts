Search by first author, or take a random one; hover (or tap) a bubble for its details and click (or tap twice) to move on to it. Bubbles are colored by track and sized by how close the match is. Degrees widen the view to neighbors of neighbors (up to 3; past that it would reach nearly every abstract). Titles and abstract text are not shown here (see how the match is made).
