Landscape of abstracts how is the map made? ›

Every ASHG abstract from 2022 to 2026 as a dot, placed so that abstracts with similar wording sit close together, on one map for all the years.

Search an author to highlight their abstracts and label them on the map (search more people to label several; the tooltip's button labels a clicked dot's first author) (a target marks the ones they lead; the diamond is the weighted center of all of them, counting first authorship most; a dashed line joins their yearly centers in order when they published in more than one year) and draw a web to their co-authors' abstracts or to the abstracts that read most like theirs, shaded by text match. Toggle years to show only some of them. Paint a keyword or an institution to see where it sits on the map. Keywords group spellings that mean the same thing (PPI and protein-protein interaction). Hover a dot for details; drag to pan and scroll or pinch to zoom. Titles and abstract text are not shown here.
