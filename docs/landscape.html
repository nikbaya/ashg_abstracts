Landscape of abstracts how is the map made? ›

Every ASHG abstract from 2022 to 2026 as a dot, placed so that abstracts with similar wording sit close together, on one map for all the years.

Search an author to show their + label and path through the years. The web and abstract-node highlights start off; choose co-authors or similar text in the author's card options to draw a web. Turn on “Show co-author abstracts” there to highlight the searched author's abstracts and, in co-authors mode, linked co-author abstracts. The card also lets you show a target on abstracts they lead. Search more people to label several; the tooltip's button labels a clicked dot's first author. Toggle years to show only some of them. Paint a keyword or an institution to see where it sits on the map. Keywords group spellings that mean the same thing (PPI and protein-protein interaction). Hover a dot for details; drag to pan and scroll or pinch to zoom. Titles and abstract text are not shown here.
