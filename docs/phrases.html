Phrases that predict a talk

Which words and phrases are over-represented in talk abstracts compared with posters? Each phrase gets an odds ratio: how much more likely an abstract containing it is to be a talk.

Phrases are 1–3-word sequences from the abstract text. Variants of one idea that turn up in mostly the same abstracts (“massively parallel reporter”, “parallel reporter assay”, “MPRA”) are grouped under the best-scoring one, with the rest listed beneath it in small type. All years pooled, phrase trends and a phrase lookup are on the overview.
