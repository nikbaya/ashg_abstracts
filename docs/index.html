Talks vs posters at ASHG

Every year the American Society of Human Genetics picks a few hundred of its several thousand accepted abstracts for a talk; the rest become posters. This site parses the published abstracts (2022–2026: 15,327 abstracts, 1,544 talks) and asks which phrases, institutions and places show up more often among talks.

Read this first. Only accepted abstracts are published, so rejected submissions never appear here, and authors can request a poster instead of a talk, so a poster does not mean an abstract was passed over for a talk. “Talk rate” is the fraction of accepted abstracts presented as a talk, not an acceptance rate or a quality score. Every number here is an association: it says how often a phrase, institution or place goes with a talk, not that it earns one (adding a phrase to your abstract will not make it a talk), and differences can reflect topic, field or what authors chose to submit. Not every affiliation could be matched to an institution or place: see the match rates.
