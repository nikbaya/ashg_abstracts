Who and what gets a talk at ASHG?

Every year the American Society of Human Genetics picks a few hundred of its several thousand accepted abstracts for a talk; the rest become posters. This site parses the published abstract booklets (2022–2026) and asks which phrases, institutions and places show up more often among talks.

Read this first. The booklets list only accepted abstracts, and authors can ask for a poster. "Talk rate" is the fraction of accepted abstracts presented as a talk, not an acceptance rate or a quality score. Every number here is an association.

Over all 5 meetings (15,327 abstracts, 1,544 talks), talks over-index on “human”, “regulatory”, “chromatin”; posters on “patient”, “case”, “literature”. Each year, phrase trends and a phrase lookup →

Talk rate by year

The share of accepted abstracts presented as a talk ranges from 8.3% to 11.9% by year. Every talk rate on this site is compared with its own year's rate, or with the pooled rate for all years.
