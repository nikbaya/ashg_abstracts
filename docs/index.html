ASHG abstracts: who and what gets a talk

Parsed from the ASHG annual meeting abstract booklets. Talks = platform + plenary presentations.

Read this first. The booklets list only accepted abstracts, and authors can ask for a poster. "Talk share" is the share of accepted abstracts presented as a talk, not an acceptance rate or a quality score. All numbers are associations.

2025Methods

ASHG 2025

3,154 abstracts, 263 of them talks. Baseline talk share 8.3%. 16 abstracts (16 talks) are cut off mid-text in the booklet itself. They count toward totals, the baseline and first-author credit, but not toward senior-author credit (the author list is cut off) or the text analyses.

Methods

  • Text extracted with PyMuPDF and split into abstracts using each booklet's layout (titles, "Authors:", "Abstract:"). Every parsed abstract is checked against the booklet's table of contents, and a hand-checked sample of 55 abstracts is used as a test set.
  • Institutions: a hand-curated list of ~150 institutions and their spelling variants. Schools fold into their university (except Harvard Medical School); NIH institutes fold into NIH; hospitals and independent institutes stay separate.
  • Senior author = last listed person (consortium names skipped). The same person = same first + last name, and for common surnames also a shared institution.
  • Phrases: 1–3-word n-grams; per phrase, a talk-vs-poster odds ratio with +0.5 smoothing and a 95% CI, ranked by z-score. A phrase is dropped from the lists when a longer phrase containing it covers ≥90% of the same abstracts.
Code: https://github.com/nikbaya/ashg_abstracts. Booklets © ASHG; only aggregate results are published here.
