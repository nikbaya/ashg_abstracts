Who and what gets a talk at ASHG?

Every year the American Society of Human Genetics picks a few hundred of its several thousand accepted abstracts for a talk; the rest become posters. This site parses the published abstracts (2022–2026: 15,327 abstracts, 1,544 talks) and asks which phrases, institutions and places show up more often among talks.

Read this first. The abstracts list only accepted work, and authors can ask for a poster. “Talk rate” is the fraction of accepted abstracts presented as a talk, not an acceptance rate or a quality score. Every number here is an association. Not every affiliation could be matched to an institution or place: see the match rates.

4The center of mass follows the host city

Local groups bring extra abstracts to a home meeting. Leave out first authors from the host city and the center of mass of North American abstracts moves 352 km for Boston 2025, and 56 km for Denver 2024.
