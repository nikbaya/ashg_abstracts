Who and what gets a talk at ASHG?

Every year the American Society of Human Genetics picks a few hundred of its several thousand accepted abstracts for a talk; the rest become posters. This site parses the published abstract booklets (2022–2025) and asks which phrases, institutions and places show up more often among talks.

Read this first. The booklets list only accepted abstracts, and authors can ask for a poster. "Talk rate" is the fraction of accepted abstracts presented as a talk, not an acceptance rate or a quality score. Every number here is an association.
